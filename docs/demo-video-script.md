# Guestimator demo video script (Devpost, about 2:30)

Record on the phone with Android's screen recorder (swipe down twice, tap Screen record, turn on "Record audio" if you narrate live). You can also record the screen silently and add the voice-over afterwards. Keep the phone in portrait.

**Before you record**
- Pick one real item you haven't listed yet, with a readable maker's mark or model number (a piece of pottery, a tool or a camera). Good light, plain background.
- Open the app with at least 2 credits: one for the estimate, one for the listing.
- eBay is already connected (the home screen says so).
- Turn on Do Not Disturb.
- Have the eBay app or eBay website ready to show the live listing at the end.

---

## 0:00 to 0:12 Hook
**Screen:** the item on the table, then the Guestimator home screen.
**Say:** "Everyone has stuff worth selling and no idea what it's worth. Guestimator prices it from a photo, using what's actually listed on eBay right now, then lists it for you on your own eBay account."

## 0:12 to 0:40 Take the photos
**Screen:** tap **📷 Guestimate something**. Tap the **Front**, **Back** and **Marks & labels** tiles and take each photo. Type a few words in the description (for example "old cast iron tool, stamped patent 1878"). Tap **✨ Guestimate it**.
**Say:** "Three photos: front, back and any marks. Plus one line about what I know. The photo alone isn't enough, so the app asks for that."

## 0:40 to 0:55 While it works
**Screen:** the "Appraising…" card. Keep recording; it takes about a minute. Cut this part down in editing.
**Say:** "It reads the marks, identifies the item, then pulls live eBay listings for the same thing."

## 0:55 to 1:25 The estimate
**Screen:** scroll the result slowly: the name and "% sure", the price range and suggested price, the blue box "N comparables listed on eBay right now", tap one comparable link and come back, then open **Set aside**.
**Say:** "Here's the range, the suggested price, and the real listings it's based on. I can tap any of them to check. It also shows the listings it threw out and why, like wrong size or wrong model. You can check the number yourself instead of taking it on trust."

## 1:25 to 1:55 Build the eBay listing
**Screen:** tap **List it on eBay**. Show the listing form: change the title slightly, adjust the price, show the category and the item specifics, and type the shipping cost.
**Say:** "One tap and it writes the eBay listing: category from eBay's own list, item specifics, condition, description and photos. Everything is editable before anything goes to eBay."

## 1:55 to 2:15 Fee preview, then list
**Screen:** tap **Preview listing & eBay fees**. Pause on the "Ready to list?" screen and the eBay fee lines. Tap **List it now**. Show "It's on eBay 🎉", then tap **View your listing** and show it live on eBay.
**Say:** "Before it costs anything, eBay itself quotes the fees. Nothing is live until I say so. List it, and it's on my eBay account."

## 2:15 to 2:30 Credits (RevenueCat)
**Screen:** go back to the home screen and tap the credits pill ("X credits left"). Show the Credits sheet with the Google Play prices.
**Say:** "It runs on credits through RevenueCat and Google Play. One credit is one estimate or one eBay listing. Pro is three hundred a month, split however you want. Guestimator: point, price, list."

**End card (text on screen, 3 seconds):** Guestimator · theguestimator.com · Get it on Google Play

---

## Editing notes
- Speed up or cut the "Appraising…" wait. Say it takes about a minute rather than showing all of it.
- Blur or crop your email address on the home screen.
- Only show things that really happened. If eBay rejects the listing, show the refund message and list something else rather than faking the success screen.
- Upload to YouTube as Unlisted or Public and paste the link into Devpost's video field.
